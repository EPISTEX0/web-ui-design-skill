# Shiftboard time-off page (client notes)

- Who uses it: the three restaurant managers, mostly on a desktop in the back office, sometimes on their phone during service.
- Show pending requests first, oldest first. Approved and declined ones can sit below or behind a filter.
- For each request we need: staff member, their location, type (vacation, sick, personal), the dates and number of days, and the note.
- Declining needs a reason, because the staff member sees it.
- Nice to have: flag when two people at the same location would be off on overlapping days.
- It must look like the rest of Shiftboard. Do not restyle the existing pages.
