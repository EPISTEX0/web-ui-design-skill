# Parc Le Goff, oyster farm, Cancale (client notes)

- Main goal of the page: people order a box of oysters for home delivery. The tide-flat visit is a secondary thing, one section lower down.
- Who visits: food lovers in Paris, Rennes and Brussels ordering for a weekend dinner or a family lunch, many over 45. Mostly on their phone, often after seeing us at the market or on a restaurant menu. English is fine for now, a French version comes later.
- Boxes: 24 creuses No. 3 EUR 32; 48 creuses No. 3 EUR 58; 12 plates (Cancale flat oysters) EUR 39. The plates are only available September to April. Delivery EUR 12, free over EUR 80. We deliver to mainland France, Belgium and Luxembourg.
- The rule people need to see before they order: we ship only on Wednesdays and Thursdays so the oysters never sit in a depot over a weekend. Orders close Monday at 18:00 for that week. They keep 7 days in the fridge, flat side up.
- Tide-flat visit with Maëlle: 2 hours on the oyster beds at low tide, EUR 25, children under 12 free, boots provided. Only on big tides, so 2027 dates are fixed: 10 April, 8 May, 5 June, 3 July, 31 July, 28 August.
- Real proof: third generation on the same beds since 1961; we supply Le Bistrot du Port and two other restaurants in Saint-Malo. No reviews collected yet.
- Brand: slate blue #3e5363, kelp ochre #c8a24a, shell white #f4efe6. My grandfather drew our oyster logo by hand; I will send it, use a placeholder for now. No photos yet.
- Would hate: black and gold luxury look, champagne stock photos, the word "artisanal" everywhere, newsletter pop-ups.
- Practical: farm shop at Port de la Houle, Cancale, open Wednesday to Sunday 10:00 to 13:00.
