Shop hours

    Questions

    When will my order ship?

    We ship on Wednesdays and Thursdays. Orders close on Monday at 18:00 for that week. An order sent after that goes out the following week.

    Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

    We deliver to mainland France, Belgium and Luxembourg. Delivery is €12, and free when your order is over €80.

    How do I pay?

    You send us your order by email. We confirm it and send you a payment link. You are not charged before that.

    How long do the oysters keep?

    7 days in the fridge, flat side up.

    Why are the plates only part of the year?

    Our Cancale flat oysters are only available from September to April.

    Can I collect my order?

    Online orders are delivered only. You are welcome at the farm shop, Port de la Houle in Cancale, Wednesday to Sunday from 10:00 to 13:00.
