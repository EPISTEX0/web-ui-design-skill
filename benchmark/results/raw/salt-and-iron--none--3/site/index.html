Harwick Maritime Museum · Exhibition guide

Salt and Iron

The sea corrodes what people make to cross it. This exhibition follows the ships, tools and lives shaped by that long contest between water and metal.

About the exhibition

A short orientation before you step into the galleries.

Theme

Two forces

Salt preserves, cures and destroys. Iron builds hulls, anchors and ambitions. Each gallery looks at where they meet.
