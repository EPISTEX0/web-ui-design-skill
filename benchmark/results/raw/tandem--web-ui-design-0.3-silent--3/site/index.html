Questions people ask.

Is a test ride free?

Yes, a test ride costs nothing and carries no obligation. Please bring ID.

Can I bring my kids or dog on the test ride?

Please do. Seeing how your own passengers settle in is the best test. Tell us their ages when you book so we can fit the right seats and belts.

Do I need to book?

Booking guarantees a bike set up for you and someone free to ride along. Walk-ins are welcome when we have a bike free.

Do you repair bikes bought elsewhere?

Ask us. We service cargo bikes and e-assist systems, and we'll tell you honestly if a job is better done elsewhere.

Where can I park a cargo bike in Utrecht?

Many cargo bikes don't fit standard racks. We'll show you the spots near home and work that do, and what to do about a narrow hallway.
