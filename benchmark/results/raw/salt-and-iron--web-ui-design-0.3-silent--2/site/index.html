    Three rooms, one argument

    The exhibition moves from the thing that destroys to the thing that is destroyed, and ends with what is left.

    Salt

    Gallery 2 · about 15 minutes

    Salt was a cargo before it was a threat. This room follows it from pan to hold: how it was made, traded and packed, and how it worked its way into everything aboard.

    Sample objects: salt crystal cluster, cooper's barrel.

    Iron

    Gallery 3 · about 20 minutes

    Iron replaced timber and changed what a ship could be. Here are the tools, fastenings and fittings that held hulls together, and the people who made them.

    Sample objects: forged anchor, hull plate, chain link.

    Rust

    Gallery 3, far end · about 10 minutes

    What the sea does when the two meet. Recovered pieces show corrosion at work, and conservators explain how it is slowed, never stopped.

    Sample objects: ship's lantern.
