The bikes

Find your fit

Three types cover most families and businesses. Not sure which? Tell us what you carry and we'll point you to one.

The Dutch classic

A big box up front, so you can see your passengers and chat on the way to school. Stable and easy to live with.

  • Room for 2–4 kids or a big shop
  • Low step-through, easy to park
  • Great with an electric motor

Long and nimble

An extended rear rack that rides almost like a normal bike. A good fit if you're coming from a regular bike or have narrow storage.

  • Carries kids, bags or both
  • Slimmer and easier to store
  • Feels familiar from the first pedal

Three wheels, no balancing

The bike stays upright when you stop, which is handy with small children, heavy loads or if balancing isn't your thing.

  • Stable at a standstill
  • Large, flat loading area
  • Popular for deliveries and businesses
