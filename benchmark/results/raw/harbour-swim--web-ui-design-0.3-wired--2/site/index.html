Membership

Pay monthly by standing order. No joining fee. Membership includes all club sessions for your category plus entry to club socials.

Junior

£18 a month

Under 16s. Lessons, development and squad places.

Adult

£32 a month

Lane swims, improvers and social sessions.

Family

£65 a month

Two adults and up to three children under 16.

Masters

£28 a month

Over 25s. Coached training, local and national meets.

Welcome

Harbour Swim Club is a friendly, volunteer run club based at Harbour Leisure Centre on Quay Road. We have around 240 members, from toddlers having their first splash to masters swimmers in their seventies.

Whether you want to learn to swim, keep fit, or race for the county, there is a session for you. New members are always welcome and your first visit is free, just bring a towel and a smile.

Join the club

Fill in the form and our membership secretary will get in touch.

Not sure yet? Come along to any session for a free trial.

Club News

12 March 2013

Cornwall County Championships: 14 medals for Harbour

Our juniors came home from Plymouth with six gold, four silver and four bronze. Well done to everyone who swam and thank you to the parents who helped on poolside.

Priya will be coaching a new masters session on Wednesday evenings starting at half past seven. All abilities, just turn up.

Pool maintenance closure over Easter weekend

The leisure centre is closing the pool from Good Friday to Easter Monday while the filters are replaced. Normal sessions resume on Tuesday.
