Species guide

Look up what you found on a foray in the Forest of Dean: when it grows, where, its spore print, and how to tell it from its lookalike.

16 species in this guide so far. The group has recorded 1,140 species in the Forest of Dean since 1987.

Read this before you use the guide

  • This guide helps members look up what they found. It cannot tell you whether a fungus is safe to eat. A description or a photo is never enough, and several of our deadliest species look like harmless ones.
  • Never eat anything because of what you read here. If you are not certain, do not eat it. Ask an experienced member to check your find in person first.
  • “Eaten by members” records what some of our members do. It is not advice and not a guarantee.
  • If someone may have eaten a poisonous or unknown fungus, call 999 or NHS 111 now and keep a piece of it.
