    How ordering works

    There is no payment on this page. You send us a request and we confirm it personally.

    1. Fill your basket

      Pick your boxes and tell us whether you want delivery or to collect. Orders close on Monday at 18:00 for that week.

    2. Send your request

      The page opens an email to us with your order filled in. Press send and it reaches us.

    3. We confirm

      We reply by email or phone to confirm your order, your day and how to pay. Then we pack it.

    Questions

    What happens after I send my request?

    We reply by email or phone to confirm your order, your delivery or pickup day, and how to pay. Your order is only confirmed once you hear from us.

    What if I miss the Monday 18:00 deadline?

    Your order goes into the following week’s shipment. The page always shows the next dates.

    How should I store my oysters?

    Keep them in the fridge, deep shell down, under a damp cloth. Not in water and not in a sealed bag. Eat them within a few days and open them just before serving.

    Why are the plates only sold part of the year?

    Cancale flat oysters are in season from September to April. The box comes back in September.

    Can I collect my order instead?

    Yes, and it’s free. Collect at the farm shop on Port de la Houle, Wednesday to Sunday from 10:00 to 13:00, or from our stand at the Saturday market.
