Questions we hear a lot

Do I need to book a test ride?

It helps, because we can have the right bike and child seats ready. If you walk in and a bike is free, we'll happily take you out.

Can I bring my kids or dog?

Please do. Riding with the real load is the whole point of a test ride.

Is an electric bike worth it?

For most families in Utrecht, yes. A loaded cargo bike is heavy, and assist makes bridges and headwinds easy. We'll let you compare both back to back.

Do you service bikes you didn't sell?

Yes, within what we can get parts for. Tell us the brand and model when you book.

Where do I park a cargo bike in the city?

We'll talk you through the options near your home and the rules for your neighbourhood when you visit.
