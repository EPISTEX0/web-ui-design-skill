Fungi of the Forest of Dean

Dean Fungus Group species guide – look up what you found on a foray.

Safety first – never eat a fungus on the strength of this guide. This is an aid to identification, not proof. Several deadly species look very like edible ones, and one mushroom can be enough to kill. Only eat something that a competent expert has identified in the hand. If you think someone has eaten something poisonous, call 999 or NHS 111 straight away and keep a sample (and any leftovers) for identification. Check the rules for the land you are on before collecting – many sites, including nature reserves and SSSIs, restrict or forbid picking.
