      Questions

      Where do you deliver?

      To mainland France, Belgium and Luxembourg. We don’t deliver to Corsica or overseas territories.

      When will my oysters arrive?

      We ship on Wednesdays and Thursdays. Orders close on Monday at 18:00 for that week, so an order placed on Tuesday ships the following week.

      How much is delivery?

      €12, and free when your boxes add up to more than €80. Collecting at the farm shop is free.

      When do I pay?

      After you send your pre-order. We confirm it by email and send you payment details, and your oysters are prepared once that is settled.

      How long do they keep?

      Seven days in the fridge, flat side up.

      When can I order flat oysters?

      The plates from Cancale are sold from September to April only. Outside those months the box is unavailable, and the cupped creuses are still on offer.

      Can I collect my order?

      Yes. Choose “Collect at the farm shop”, pick a day, and come to Port de la Houle between 10:00 and 13:00, Wednesday to Sunday.
