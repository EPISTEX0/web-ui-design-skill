About the exhibition

[One or two sentences from the curatorial statement.] Salt and Iron follows two cargoes from shore to sea: one preserved the catch and the crew, the other built and armed the ship.

Salt

Preservation, trade and the economies of the coast: how salt turned a perishable catch into a global commodity.

Iron

Anchors, fastenings, cannon and chain: the metal that held ships together and the foundries that made it.

People

Salters, smiths, coopers and crews. The lives behind the objects on display.
