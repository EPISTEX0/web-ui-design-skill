Species guide

Look up what you found on a foray. Search by name, or narrow it down by month, where it grew and its shape.

This guide names fungi. It does not tell you what is safe to eat.

Many edible species have deadly look-alikes, and a photo or a description is not enough to be sure. Never eat a fungus because of what you read here. Have an experienced person check it in the hand.

If you think someone has eaten a poisonous fungus, call 999 or NHS 111 straight away and keep a sample of what was eaten.
