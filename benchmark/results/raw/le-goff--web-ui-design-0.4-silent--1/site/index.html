Pick a calibre, then a box

Oysters are sold by size. The number is the calibre: the lower the number, the bigger the oyster. Prices change with the calibre you pick.

Calibre

Sizes are the weight of one oyster, in shell, as is usual for Pacific oysters.

How ordering works

We harvest for your order, so we confirm each one by hand before anything leaves the water.

  1. Choose your boxes

    Pick a calibre and add boxes to your order. You can mix sizes.

  2. Send your request

    Tell us who you are and when you want them. The request opens in your email.

  3. We confirm

    We reply to confirm the day and the final price, usually within a day.

  4. Collect or receive

    Pick up at the farm or the market, or have them delivered nearby. You pay then.

Keeping your oysters

        
    
          
  • Take them out of the box when you get home and keep them in the fridge, flat side up.
    • 
          
  • Cover them with a damp cloth. Never put them in water, and never in a sealed bag.
    • 
          
  • Eat them within a few days of collecting. The sooner, the better.
    • 
        

      

      

        
Opening them

        
    
          
  • Hold the oyster flat side up in a folded cloth, hinge towards you.
    • 
          
  • Work the tip of an oyster knife into the hinge, twist, then slide along the top shell.
    • 
          
  • Keep the juice in the shell. Serve with lemon, or a little shallot vinegar.
