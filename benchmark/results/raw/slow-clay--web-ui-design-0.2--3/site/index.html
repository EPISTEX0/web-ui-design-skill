Six weeks, start to finished pots

The course follows the life of a pot, from a lump on the wheel to something you can use at home.

  1. Centering

    Wedging, setting up at the wheel and getting clay to run true.

  2. Opening and pulling

    Opening the form and drawing up the first cylinders.

  3. Cylinders and bowls

    Refining walls and learning how a bowl differs from a cup.

  4. Shaping and trimming

    Finishing the foot and cleaning up what you threw.

  5. Glazing

    Choosing glazes and applying them to your pieces.

  6. Firing and collecting

    Your work goes through the kiln and comes home with you.
