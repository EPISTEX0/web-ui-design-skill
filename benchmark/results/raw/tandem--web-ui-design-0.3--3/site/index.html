Questions we often get.

Is the test ride really free?

Yes. The test ride is free and lasts 30 minutes, and you're under no obligation to buy.

Can I bring my kids?

Please do. Trying the bike with the people who'll actually ride in it is the best way to know it fits.

Can I lease a bike?

Yes, leasing starts from €89 a month. Ask us on your test ride and we'll talk you through the options.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00, at Oudegracht 112 in Utrecht. We're closed on Sunday and Monday.
