Read this before you eat anything

  • This guide helps you put a name to a find. It cannot make a fungus safe to eat.
  • Never eat a fungus unless you are certain what it is. If you are not certain, do not eat it.
  • Several species here have a deadly look-alike. Check every feature, not just one or two.
  • Poisoning: call 999 or NHS 111 now and keep a piece of the mushroom.
