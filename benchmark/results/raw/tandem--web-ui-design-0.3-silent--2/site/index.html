Common questions

Is a cargo bike hard to ride?

It takes a little getting used to, mostly in turns and when stopping. Most people feel at home within a quarter of an hour, which is why a test ride comes first.

Do I need an appointment for a test ride?

We recommend it. The type you want is ready and we have time for you. Use the form above.

Can I bring my own children?

Yes, that's the point. You see whether seats, belts and space work for your family.

Do you have electric models?

Ask us what's in stock right now. Electric assist makes heavy loads and Utrecht's bridges a lot easier.
