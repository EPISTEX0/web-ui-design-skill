Do not eat a fungus on the strength of this page

Several of the most dangerous species here look like good edibles. Have anything you plan to eat identified in person by an experienced member, and check Forestry England’s rules before you collect.

    This guide covers a selection of the fungi most often met on Dean Fungus Group forays. It is an aid to naming what you found, not a licence to eat it. Many species are variable, and some deadly ones are small and plain.
