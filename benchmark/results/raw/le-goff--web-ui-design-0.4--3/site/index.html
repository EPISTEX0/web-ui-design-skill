Questions

      

        
How do I pay?
Send your order and we reply by email to confirm it and to arrange payment. Nothing is charged when you send the form.

        
What if I miss the Monday cutoff?
Your order is still welcome. It goes out in the next posting week, on Wednesday or Thursday. The summary tells you the exact dates.

        
Where do you deliver?
Mainland France, Belgium and Luxembourg. Delivery costs EUR 12 and is free when your order is over EUR 80.

        
Can I collect my online order?
No. Online orders are posted. You can buy in person at the farm shop at Port de la Houle, Wednesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 13:00.

        
When are plates available?
Plates, the flat oysters, are sold from September to April. The plates box is switched off the rest of the year.

        
How should I keep them until I eat them?
Keep the oysters somewhere cool, ideally in the fridge, with the cupped side down. Do not put them in water and do not freeze them. Open them just before eating.
