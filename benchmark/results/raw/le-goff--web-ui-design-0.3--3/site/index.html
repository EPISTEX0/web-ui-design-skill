    Questions

    Where do you deliver?

    Mainland France, Belgium and Luxembourg. Delivery costs €12 and is free over €80.

    When do you ship?

    On Wednesdays and Thursdays. Orders for a week close on Monday at 18:00. An order sent after that goes out the following week.

    How do I pay?

    You send us your order, and we reply to confirm it and take payment. Nothing is charged on this page.

    How long do the oysters keep?

    7 days in the fridge, flat side up.

    Why can't I order plates in summer?

    Our Cancale flat oysters (plates) are only available from September to April. The creuses are available all year.

    Can I collect my order?

    Yes. Choose collection in the order form and pick up at our farm shop at Port de la Houle, Wednesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 13:00.
