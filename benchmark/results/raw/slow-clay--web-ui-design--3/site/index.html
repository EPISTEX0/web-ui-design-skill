What six weeks at the wheel looks like

A typical arc for a first wheel course. Your instructor will tailor the pace to the group.

  1. Centering and openingGetting the clay to sit still, then making the first hollow.
  2. Pulling wallsRaising a cylinder with even thickness.
  3. Shaping formsCups, bowls, and the curve in between.
  4. TrimmingGiving each piece a foot and a finished base.
  5. Handles and detailsPulled handles, rims, and small decisions.
  6. Finishing upFinal pieces, and what happens in the kiln.
