Three ways to carry

Every cargo bike we stock is electric-assist, so a loaded bike still feels light on the Utrecht bridges. Pick by what you carry and where you park.

Long-john

Two wheels, box at the front

The classic Dutch bakfiets. Kids sit in front where you can talk to them, and the low box takes groceries, a dog or a pile of boxes. It rides like a long bicycle once you’re moving.

  • Best for kids and shopping
  • Stands on its kickstand
  • Needs a wide hallway or a bike shed

Cargo trike

Three wheels, stays upright

It holds itself up at red lights and when you’re loading, which helps with heavy or wobbly cargo. Worth choosing for market stalls, deliveries and anyone who’d rather not balance.

  • Best for heavy and awkward loads
  • No balancing at a standstill
  • Widest of the three

Longtail

Two wheels, extended rear rack

Barely longer than a normal bike, so it fits a canal-house hallway or a standard bike rack. Kids ride on the back, bags hang on the sides. The smart pick when space is tight.

  • Best for narrow houses
  • Most like a normal bike
  • Smaller load than a box
