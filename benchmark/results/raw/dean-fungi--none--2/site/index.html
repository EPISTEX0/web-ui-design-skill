Forest of Dean Species Guide

Dean Fungus Group – look up what you found on a foray

Never eat a fungus on the strength of this page. This guide is a starting point for naming what you found, not a reliable way to identify it. Several deadly species look very like edible ones. Only eat a fungus if you have identified it with certainty from a specimen, ideally checked by an expert, and always try a small cooked amount first. If someone may have eaten something dangerous, call 999 or NHS 111 straight away and take a sample with you.
