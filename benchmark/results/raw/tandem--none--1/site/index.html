Find your type

Different jobs need different bikes. Here are the main types we can help you choose between. Ask us what's currently in the shop.

Most popular

Front-loader (bakfiets)

The classic Dutch box bike with the cargo area in front, so you can see and talk to your passengers.

  • Great for 1–3 kids
  • Easy to park and handle

Long-tail

A stretched rear rack that feels like a normal bike but carries children or bags behind you.

  • Slim, fits in tighter spaces
  • Good for commuting plus kids

Trikes & business bikes

Three wheels for stability with heavy loads, standing still, or when balance is a concern.

  • Stable at a stop
  • Boxes and platforms for work

How it works

  1. Tell us what you carry

    Kids, cargo, distances, where you park at night. A short chat narrows the options.

  2. Take a test ride

    Nothing replaces riding one. Try a few types and see what feels right.

  3. Ride away, we keep it running

    We help with setup and handover, and we're here for servicing and repairs afterwards.
