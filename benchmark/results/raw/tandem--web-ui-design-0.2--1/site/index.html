Three ways to carry more

Every household is different. These are the main types of cargo bike, and what each one does best. Ratings are general guidance, not specific models.

Front-box bike

The classic Dutch bakfiets. A box between the handlebars and the rider holds kids, shopping or a dog, and you can see them the whole ride. Stable when parked, and easy to load.

Longtail

A stretched rear rack that looks like a normal bike. Carries a child or two on the back, plus panniers for groceries or work gear. A good fit for narrow streets and shared storage.

Three-wheeler

Two wheels up front or at the back, and a deck that stays level at a standstill. Steady for heavy loads, young passengers and riders who prefer not to balance at traffic lights.
