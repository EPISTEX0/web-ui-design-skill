    Getting a reliable identification

    • Photograph and keep the whole fungus, including the base. Dig it up rather than cutting it. A buried cup is how a death cap gives itself away.
    • Check the underside, the stem (ring, bulb, net or scales), the smell, and what changes colour when cut.
    • Note the trees nearby and whether it grew on soil or wood.
    • Bring anything unusual to the recorder at the end of the foray.

    About this guide

    These are common species of the kind found in the woods, grass and verges of the Forest of Dean. The sketches are schematic colour guides, not photographs, and one fungus varies a great deal in life. Dates show the usual fruiting months in southern Britain and shift with the weather.

    Check the rules for the land you are on before picking anything.
