Dean Fungus Group · Fungi of the Forest of Dean. Look up what you found on a foray.

Read this before you eat anything.
  • This guide helps you name what you found. It is not a licence to eat it. Several deadly species look like edible ones.
  • Never eat a fungus you cannot identify with 100% certainty from several features, ideally checked by an experienced person.
  • If someone may have eaten something poisonous, call 999 or NHS 111 at once, even if they feel fine. Symptoms of the worst poisonings can be delayed by many hours. Keep a sample of the fungus.
  • Check Forestry England's current rules before collecting. Many sites and protected areas restrict picking, and commercial collecting is not allowed.
