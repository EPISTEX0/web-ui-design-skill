## Direction
Archival catalogue feel. Ground is salt (cool grey-white, not cream), text is tarred iron, the single accent is iron oxide rust. Signature: the floor plan as a ruled room grid that doubles as navigation, plus a "mark as seen" tally and a Low light toggle for dim rooms.
