# Skerra Ferry Company, MV Gannet loading screen (client notes)

- Who uses it: the loading master and two deckhands, on a rugged Android tablet in a waterproof case, held in portrait. Outdoors on the slipway in rain, wind and low winter sun, usually wearing thick gloves. Signal at Port Ailig drops out, so it has to keep working and catch up later.
- Main job: as each vehicle drives up, find its booking by number plate (they only want to type the last 3 characters), mark it loaded and give it a lane. See at a glance how much deck space is left.
- The deck: 4 lanes, 22 car units in total. Lanes 1 and 4 hold 5 units, lanes 2 and 3 hold 6. A car is 1 unit, a van or car with trailer 2, a lorry or the island bus 4 and only in lanes 2 or 3.
- The thing every new crew member gets wrong: lane 1 runs under the passenger lounge and has only 2.1 m headroom. Anything over 2.1 m, including cars with roof boxes, must never go in lane 1.
- Boarding order: priority-badge island residents and the doctor's and vet's vans first, then other bookings, then standby. Standby cars wait in the shore lane in arrival order and can be called on once booked cars are loaded. Ten minutes before departure, booked no-shows lose their space to standby.
- Route: Port Ailig to Skerra, 25 minutes. Winter departures from Port Ailig: 07:10, 08:30, 10:15, 12:40, 14:30, 16:20, 18:05. On Thursdays the 10:15 carries only the fuel tanker plus foot passengers.
- Brand: navy #12304a and lifejacket yellow #f2b705. It needs strong contrast for glare. No logo file needed on this screen.
- Hate: tiny checkboxes, typing full plates with gloves on, anything that freezes when signal drops, and the clipboard going soggy.
