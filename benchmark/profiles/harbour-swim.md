# Harbour Swim Club website (client notes)

- Who uses it: parents and older members, mostly on their phones, often poolside, mainly to check the timetable and prices.
- Keep our club colours, navy and yellow, and the logo. Members recognise them.
- Keep every session in the timetable, the prices, the join form and the news.
- Volunteers edit the site by hand, so it must stay plain HTML, CSS and JS with no build step or framework.
- Scope: make it modern and easy to use on phones, but it should still feel like our club. A refresh, not a different club.
